Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Zenith Automations ("Zenith Automations," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit zenith-automations.com, submit a form, or communicate with us by phone, text message (SMS), or email, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company name, website, industry, the services you're interested in, budget, timeline, and any details you share in forms, emails, calls or messages.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited and approximate location, which may be collected through standard website logs and analytics tools.
  • Client project data: when you hire us, we may access your business software (for example, GoHighLevel and connected tools) only as needed to deliver the services you request.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and send you a project plan or proposal
  • To schedule calls and provide the services you request
  • To send appointment reminders, project updates and customer-care messages
  • To send marketing messages, only where you have given consent
  • To improve our website and services, and to meet legal obligations

3. SMS / text messaging

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, Zenith Automations may send you text messages about your inquiry, appointments, project updates and, where you've agreed, offers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at anna@zenith-automations.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers that help us deliver messages (for example, our messaging platform). Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, form processing, email, CRM and messaging platforms, bound to use it only on our behalf
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights
  • A successor business if Zenith Automations is involved in a merger or sale

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you marketing messages, by emailing anna@zenith-automations.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have additional rights under applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
